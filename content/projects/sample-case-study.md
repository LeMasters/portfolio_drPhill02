One or two sentences that say what the project is and why it is worth reading about.

## The problem

What were you asked to make, or what did you set out to make? What constraints did you have: time, team, tools, audience?

## My role

What you did, and what others did. Be specific; a reader wants to know which decisions were yours.

## Process

How the work went: sketches, prototypes, playtests, the moments the design changed and why. Images help here.

## Results

What you made, and how people responded to it. Numbers if you have them.

## What I learned

What you would do differently, and what you now do in every project because of this one.
